FAQs

Engitech Expo Ahmedabad > FAQs
General

General

1. What is Engitech Expo 2026?
Engitech Expo 2026 is a leading industrial exhibition that brings together manufacturers, suppliers, technology providers, and industry professionals from engineering and allied sectors under one roof.
2. When and where will Engitech Expo 2026 be held?

Engitech Expo 2026 will be held in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

3. Who should visit Engitech Expo 2026?
  • Industrial manufacturers
  • Business owners & entrepreneurs
  • Purchase managers
  • Engineers & technicians
  • Distributors & dealers
  • Startups in the engineering sector
4. Which industries will be covered at Engitech Expo 2026?
  • Industrial machinery & tools
  • Automation & instrumentation
  • Electrical & electronics
  • Welding & fabrication
  • Material handling
  • Power, energy & allied sectors
Exhibitor

Exhibitor

1. Why should companies exhibit at Engitech Expo 2026?

Exhibiting at Engitech Expo 2026 helps companies:

  • Showcase products to a targeted B2B audience
  • Generate quality business leads
  • Build brand visibility
  • Connect with distributors and buyers
  • Explore partnerships and collaborations
2. How can I register as an exhibitor?

You can register as an exhibitor by contacting the Engitech Expo team through:

  • Website: www.engitechexpo.com
  • Email: info@engitechexpo.com
  • Phone: +91 96019 45255
Visitor

Visitor

1. Is visitor registration required for Engitech Expo 2026?

Yes, visitors are encouraged to register in advance to ensure smooth entry and receive updates about the event.

2. Is there any entry fee for visitors?

Visitor entry details and charges (if any) will be mentioned on the official website closer to the event date.

3. Will there be live product demonstrations?

Yes, many exhibitors will conduct live product demonstrations and showcase the latest technologies during the expo. 

4. Can startups participate in Engitech Expo 2026?

Yes, Engitech Expo 2026 welcomes startups and emerging companies looking to showcase innovations and connect with industry leaders.
